Implied

A quiet puzzle game built from seven optical illusions. A figure made of broken circles walks on bridges that exist only because your eyes imply them. Each chapter turns a real illusion into a rule: implied edges, fading sight, size by surroundings, viewing angle, change blindness, impossible stairs and delayed feedback.

Help

Stuck? After a while on a puzzle, or after restarting it twice, a “?” appears in the bottom left. It plays the solution.

Tilting: in chapters IV and VI, hold your phone as you like when the puzzle opens; that is “level”. Tilt from there.

Progress is saved on your device automatically.
