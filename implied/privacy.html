Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

In short: Implied does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your progress stays on your device.

Who we are

Implied is a puzzle game for iPhone developed by Alperen Kolaylı (“we”). This policy explains how the game handles your information.

Information we collect

None. The game has no user accounts and sends nothing to us or to anyone else. It contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or other third-party data-collection tools, and it does not use the internet.

Information stored on your device

The game saves which puzzles you have solved and which chapter introductions you have seen, locally inside its own storage on your device. Deleting the game deletes this data.

Motion sensors

Some chapters let you tilt your phone. The game reads the device’s motion sensors only while you play, only on your device, and never records or sends this information anywhere.

Children

The game collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
